Beneath the sun, the boundary gleams,
A patch of turf where nations dream.
In pristine whites or colors bold,
An ancient story gets retold—
With leather stitched in crimson thread,
And silent prayers left unsaid.

The bowler marks a measured stride,
With roaring crowds on every side.
A sudden sprint, a leap of grace,
To hurl the sphere through open space—
It swings through air, it grips the seam,
To shatter hope and break the dream.

The batter stands with steady gaze,
Through spinning webs and scorching pace.
A flick of wrist, a crack of willow,
The rising roar, a rolling billow—
The ball goes racing past the green,
The sweetest sound that's ever seen.

Or leaping high against the sky,
As wooden bails go flying by—
A finger raised, a sudden hush,
Followed by a jubilant rush.
From dusty streets and gullies tight,
To stadiums ablaze with light.

Five days of grit or twenty thrills,
A test of nerve, a clash of wills.
More than a game of bat and ball,
It holds a billion hearts in thrall.
